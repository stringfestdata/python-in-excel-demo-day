## The three demos

1. **A single cell that surprises** — your first PY() cell and the calc-order rule.
2. **Summarize what formulas can't** — `describe()`, `value_counts()`, `groupby()`.
3. **A chart formulas can't draw** — a one-line seaborn heatmap.

The instructor script paces these over 35 minutes, with 10 minutes for the CTA block and 10 for Q&A.
